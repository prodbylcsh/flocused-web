First launch: macOS can't verify the app

Flocused isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it.

  1. Open Flocused. When macOS says it can't verify the app, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Flocused.
  3. Confirm with Open Anyway. From then on, Flocused opens normally.

On macOS 13 and 14 you can instead Control-click the app and choose Open, then Open. Repeat these steps whenever you install a newer build.

Build from source

Requires a Mac with the Xcode command line tools.

bash scripts/build-app.sh

This creates a universal build/Flocused.app and build/Flocused.zip. Every push also builds the app on a Mac build machine and publishes it as the latest build.
